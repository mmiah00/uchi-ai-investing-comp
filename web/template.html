Contract Manufacturer · Precision Optics

Optical packaging for the networks running AI

Fabrinet designs and manufactures precision optical, electro-mechanical, and electronic products under contract for some of the world's largest technology companies. Headquartered in the Cayman Islands with its principal manufacturing base in Thailand, it packages and tests the optical transceivers, lasers, and sensors used in telecom and datacenter networks — including the high-speed optics behind AI infrastructure buildouts — alongside automotive, industrial, and medical-device work.
