SHORT
Fabrinet (FN) — priced like an AI-optics compounder, filings say otherwise
—

1Thesis

The market prices FN's rising AI/datacenter exposure like a margin-expanding compounder. Its own filings show the opposite: gross margin peaked in FY2023 (12.7%) and has declined for three straight years to 12.0% despite record revenue, and FY2026's $473.0mm of net income produced only ~$3–4mm of conventional free cash flow. This is deliberately not the customer-concentration short — concentration is genuinely diversifying (Nvidia's share fell from 35.1% to 16.3%, replaced by share-gainers). The case rests entirely on margin structure and cash conversion.

2Why FN, not COHR or LITE

All three were screened as short/long candidates on the same AI-optics buildout. FN's forward multiple (—) is actually the lowest of the three — this isn't a "FN is the rich one" argument. It's a quality gap the market hasn't fully priced: FN shows none of the margin or cash-flow improvement its peers do, yet still trades in the same broad multiple neighborhood.

3Three pillars

01
No pricing power despite mix shift.Incremental gross margin (profit on each new dollar of revenue) has run below the average margin in 3 of the last 4 years — the opposite of what a genuine AI-mix re-rating should show.
02
Cash conversion has collapsed.$473.0mm of net income → ~$3mm of FCF, down from $364.7mm two years earlier — driven by inventory nearly doubling (+75.8%, concentrated in raw materials) and capex more than quintupling ($47.5mm→$252.5mm cash-paid).
03
Valuation still requires an unprecedented FCF margin.Even after the ~48% decline and EV/EBITDA compression from ~38x to ~21x, a reverse-DCF grid requires FN to sustain a 10.9%–27.9% FCF margin indefinitely — a range that starts at its best-ever result (12.6%, FY2024) against an actual FY2026 margin of ~0.1%.

5Risks — not softened

  • Timing risk. Stock is already down ~48% from its 52-week high and EV/EBITDA has already compressed ~38x→21x — much of the "easy" re-rating may already be done.
  • No sell-side confirmation yet. JPMorgan, Barclays, Needham and Rosenblatt all held or raised targets on the day (Aug 18, 2026) the FCF-collapse print came out.
  • Consensus is bullish. Average price target $734.11 implies ~90% upside — real squeeze risk against this bullish a consensus.
  • Balance-sheet cushion. Zero debt at FY2026 fiscal year-end and $875.1mm cash remove the classic forced-capital-raise catalyst.
  • Precedent for normalization. FY2022-24 also saw FCF/NI dips before normalizing — this could resolve the same way once the Thailand/California buildout completes.
Bottom line: genuinely two-sided, not a one-way bet — the edge is in which assumptions each outcome requires (Bear = FN's own worst-ever observations across the board; Bull = its best-ever, simultaneously), not in direction alone. What would invalidate this: gross margin inflecting up 100bp+ for two consecutive quarters despite rising mix, or FCF margin recovering toward FY2024's ~12.6% within 2-3 quarters.
Contract Manufacturer · Precision Optics

Optical packaging for the networks running AI

Fabrinet designs and manufactures precision optical, electro-mechanical, and electronic products under contract for some of the world's largest technology companies. Headquartered in the Cayman Islands with its principal manufacturing base in Thailand, it packages and tests the optical transceivers, lasers, and sensors used in telecom and datacenter networks — including the high-speed optics behind AI infrastructure buildouts — alongside automotive, industrial, and medical-device work.
